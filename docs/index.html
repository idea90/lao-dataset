Syntax & Word Order ໂຄງສ້າງປະໂຫຍກ (ປະທານ + ກິລິຍາ + ກຳ)

Canonical SVO Word Order

Lao is strictly an SVO (Subject-Verb-Object) language. Modifiers follow the head word they modify.

Formula: Subject + Verb + Object
Example:
ຂ້ອຍກິນເຂົ້າ (Khoy kin khao)
"I eat rice."
Noun Phrase Structure ໂຄງສ້າງວະລີນາມ

Head-Initial Noun Phrases

In Lao, adjectives and possessives follow the noun they modify.

Formula: Noun + Adjective / Possessive
Example:
ຫມາໃຫຍ່ (Ma nyai)
"Big dog"
Classifiers (ລັກສະນະນາມ) ການໃຊ້ລັກສະນະນາມກັບຈຳນວນນັບ

Counting and Specification with Classifiers

Numbers cannot modify nouns directly in Lao. They require a classifier matching the semantic category of the noun. Pattern: [Noun] + [Numeral] + [Classifier]. For demonstratives: [Noun] + [Classifier] + [Demonstrative].

Formula: Noun + Number + Classifier OR Noun + Classifier + Demonstrative (ນີ້/ນັ້ນ)
Example:
ຫມາ ສອງ ໂຕ (Ma song to)
"Two dogs"
Tense & Aspect Markers ຄຳຊ່ວຍບອກການ ແລະ ລັກສະນະ

Aspectual Particles (Past, Continuous, Future)

Lao verbs do not conjugate for tense. Temporal aspects are marked with pre-verbal or post-verbal particles: ໄດ້ (past/achieved), ແລ້ວ (already/completed), ກຳລັງ/ພວມ (progressive), ຢູ່ (continuous), ຈະ/ຊິ (future).

Formula: Past: ໄດ້ + Verb (or Verb + ແລ້ວ) | Progressive: ກຳລັງ + Verb (+ ຢູ່) | Future: ຈະ/ຊິ + Verb
Example:
ຂ້ອຍໄດ້ໄປວຽງຈັນ (Khoy dai pai Viengchan)
"I went to Vientiane."
Negation ການປະຕິເສດ

Negation Particle ບໍ່ (Bor)

To negate a predicate, place ບໍ່ (bor) directly before the verb or adjective. For imperative prohibitions, use ຢ່າ (ya).

Formula: ບໍ່ + Verb / Adjective OR ຢ່າ + Verb
Example:
ຂ້ອຍບໍ່ຮູ້ (Khoy bor hu)
"I don't know."
Interrogatives (Questions) ປະໂຫຍກຄຳຖາມ

Question Particle Formation

Yes/no questions place ບໍ່ (bor) at the end. Question words (wh-words) remain in situ (in the natural position of the information requested).

Formula: Statement + ບໍ່? OR [SVO with wh-word in place]
Example:
ເຈົ້າສະບາຍດີບໍ່? (Jao sabai dee bor?)
"How are you? / Are you well?"
Nominalization (ການສ້າງຄຳນາມ) ການປ່ຽນຄຳກິລິຍາ ແລະ ຄຸນນາມ ເປັນຄຳນາມ

Prefixes ການ- (Kan-) and ຄວາມ- (Khuam-)

Verbs of action take the prefix ການ- (kan-) to form nouns (actions/activities). Adjectives, states, or emotions take the prefix ຄວາມ- (khuam-) to form abstract nouns.

Formula: ການ + Action Verb | ຄວາມ + Adjective/State
Example:
ການຮຽນ (Kan hian)
"Education / Study / Learning"
Phonology & Tone Determination ກົດເກນການຜັນສຽງວັນນະຍຸດ

Consonant Class Tone Matrix

In Lao, the tone of a syllable is determined by 3 factors: (1) Consonant Class (High, Mid, Low), (2) Syllable type (Live vs Dead, Vowel length), and (3) Tone mark (ໄມ້ເອກ, ໄມ້ໂທ).

Formula: Consonant Class + Syllable Ending + Tone Mark -> Resulting Tone (1 of 6 tones)
Example:
ກາ (Middle + Live + No mark) (Kaa)
"Tone: Low tone"
